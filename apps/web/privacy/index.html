Practice++

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Practice++ is operated by Ryan Zhou. This policy explains how information is handled when you use https://practiceplusplus.com. Contact ryan.zhou2005@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Account information and Google sign-in

You can sign in through Google or GitHub using Supabase Auth. When you sign in with Google, Google provides basic account information, including an account identifier, email address, name, and profile image when available. Supabase processes this information to authenticate you and maintain your account. Practice++ links your practice records to your authenticated account identifier.

Google information is used to sign you in, identify your account, and support account security and support requests. Practice++ does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, calendar, or contacts. It does not receive your Google or GitHub password.

Practice information

Practice++ stores the settings, goals, teaching preferences, problem attempts, outcomes, timing, notes, review schedules, daily progress, and learning summaries or memories you create or save. This information supports your practice plans, review reminders within the app, progress views, and personalized learning features.

Optional AI features

When you use integrated AI features, your messages, any code you paste, and relevant practice context are sent through the Practice++ server to the selected provider, currently OpenAI, using the API key you supply. The provider handles requests under its own terms and privacy policy. Practice++ does not use your Google account identity as a credential for this AI service.

Practice++ does not persist full AI chat transcripts or pasted source code in its application database. It does save compact learning checkpoints and evidence-backed memories generated during integrated conversations. You can inspect and manage these records in the app. If you export context to an external AI service, the information you choose to share is handled by that service.

Your AI API key stays in tab memory by default. If you choose to remember it, it is stored in this browser's local storage. The key is sent with requests that need it and is not stored in the application database. Forgetting the key or signing out clears the remembered copy through the app's key-management flow.

Browser storage and technical information

The app uses browser storage for authentication sessions, appearance preferences, and optional remembered API keys. Hosting, security, and monitoring services may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and errors. Application logging and error reporting are designed to exclude authentication credentials, AI prompts and responses, and pasted source code.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Cloudflare and Google Cloud provide hosting and delivery infrastructure. Configured error-monitoring services process sanitized diagnostic information. Optional integrated AI requests are processed by OpenAI. These services process information as needed to provide their functions.

Practice++ does not sell personal information or use Google account information for advertising. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect the service and its users.

Retention and your choices

Account and practice records are retained to provide your ongoing practice history and personalized features. You can edit supported records and remove learning memories and checkpoints through the app. Use Delete account in the app to permanently remove your sign-in account and personal practice data. Administrator accounts and accounts that own shared catalog records require assistance with deletion. To request access, correction, export, or help with deletion, email ryan.zhou2005@gmail.com.

After deletion, we retain only a minimal deletion record containing your old Supabase user ID and deletion time to prevent old or concurrent requests from recreating your profile. This record does not contain practice history or Google profile information. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account. Information required for legal or security purposes may need to be retained. Copies in backups may remain until those backups are replaced in their normal rotation. Disconnecting Practice++ in your Google account stops that authorization but does not by itself delete stored Practice++ records.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The current policy and its update date will be available on this page.
